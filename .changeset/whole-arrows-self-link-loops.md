Directed links draw as whole arrows, and self-links draw as a loop at their node (#542, #505).

- **One shape per directed link.** A directed `line` link is ONE shape: its shaft ends at the head's base and the head finishes it, on WebGL, Canvas and SVG and in `toSVG()`, with LOD off and on (leaf links and super-edges). A translucent link no longer darkens where the head overlapped the shaft. The head is `2·arrowSize` long and `w/2 + 2√w` wide on each side of its axis (half the line width `w` plus `2√w`), its tip on the target node's rim. On a bent link the shaft follows the curve into the head, and the head points along the curve's own end tangent.
- **Self-link loops.** A self-link draws as a loop at its node, starting and ending at the node's centre like an ordinary link (the node fill hides the part inside): a drop whose two legs leave and come back radially, with a round far end about 2.3 node radii out. It is placed opposite the mean direction of the node's neighbours. Several self-link edges on one node are summed into one loop, whose width, colour and flow come from the summed weight. A directed loop is one shape with its head, its tip on the rim pointing into the node. Loops follow `linkStyle` (`line`, with a head when `directed`, and `half-arrow`) and `sizeMode`, on every backend and in `toSVG()`. With LOD on, drawn leaf nodes keep their loops and aggregates draw none; in a state network the state view and the both view draw loops.
- **Tunable loop shape (provisional names).** `selfLinkSpread` sets the angle between a loop's two legs (degrees, default 40) and `selfLinkLength` its leg length (node radii, default 3). Changing either is a style change, rebuilt once, with no per-frame cost.
- **Interaction.** Hovering or clicking a loop reports the node's first self-link edge with the summed weight; selection and hover highlight include it. A head pixel picks its link.
- **Cost.** A graph without self-links pays one scan of its edges per graph and nothing per frame. A whole arrow adds 4 bytes per link (the target radius) over a line, uploaded once per style.

**Breaking (low-level instanced layers).** The instanced link primitives are now `lines` (headless lines), `arrows` (whole arrows, shaft + head as one shape) and `half-arrows` (one-sided arrows). The heads-only `arrows` primitive is gone: `InstancedArrowsData` now describes whole arrows (every `InstancedLinesData` field plus `arrowSize` and the target `radii`; `sizes` and `half` are removed), `arrowsToDrawables` exports them, and the `InstancedArrows` WebGL class is removed (whole arrows run on `InstancedLines`). Migration: draw a directed link as ONE `arrows` layer, `{ primitive: "arrows", arrows: { ...lineData, arrowSize, radii } }`, in place of a `lines` layer plus a heads-only `arrows` layer; the head size is one `arrowSize` per layer instead of a per-arrow `sizes` column. Self-link loops are their own primitive, `loops` (`InstancedLoopsData`): per loop its node's `centers`, its two rim ends as `startOffsets` / `endOffsets` from the centre, `widths` and `colors`, with one `legLength` (the cubic arms, in loop radii), one `spread` (radians) and one `head` per layer: `{ head: "none", stubLength }`, `{ head: "arrow", arrowSize }` or `{ head: "half" }`; `loopsToDrawables` exports them. The link primitives describe links only. The `network()` API is unchanged.

**Breaking (Canvas/SVG and `toSVG()` output).** A directed `line` link is now a filled `<path>` (its arrow outline) in the `links` group, no longer a stroked path with its head in the `arrows` group; the `arrows` group stays registered but is always empty. In `sizeMode: "screen"`, Canvas/SVG and `toSVG()` bake each directed link's outline at the current zoom and re-bake it when a zoom ends (as half-arrows already were). Migration: CSS or SVG post-processing that styled directed links through `stroke` / `stroke-width` on the `links` group, or selected the `arrows` group, should target `fill` on the `links` group instead.
