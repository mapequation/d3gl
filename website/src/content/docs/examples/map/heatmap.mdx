A synthetic value field on a grid, colored with `interpolateViridis` and **clipped to the land
outline**. Hover any cell to read its value, scroll to zoom, and drag the **cell-size** slider to
change grid resolution (1° ≈ 65k cells — a WebGL stress test; SVG is slower at scale). The geometry
is projected once regardless of backend.
