One map exercising every GeoJSON geometry type — `MultiPolygon` (land), `Polygon` (the green
region), `MultiLineString` (graticule), `LineString` (the route), `Point` (cities), and
`MultiPoint` (the cluster) — plus engine-managed `map.labels(...)` for the city names, which tracks
zoom and appears in `toSVG()`/`toPNG()` export.
