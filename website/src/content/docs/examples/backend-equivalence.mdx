A flower of opaque, overlapping discs with thick white borders, rendered in all three
backends at once. Because the discs overlap and are drawn in order, each disc's border is
partly covered by later discs' fills — a sensitive probe of how a backend composites
overlapping fills and strokes. WebGL, Canvas, and SVG should all agree. Zoom/pan any panel
to inspect the overlaps; the view mirrors across all three.
