The same scene rendered in all three backends at once, to check they composite identically.
Two probes:

- **Overlapping bordered shapes** — a flower of opaque, overlapping discs with thick white
  borders. Because the discs overlap and are drawn in order, each disc's border is partly
  covered by later discs' fills — a sensitive probe of fill/stroke draw order.
- **Stroke joins & caps** — thick polylines with sharp, acute, and closed corners, probing
  join style and the miter limit (where backends used to differ: bevel vs miter, and miter
  limit 10 vs 4).

WebGL, Canvas, and SVG should agree on both. Zoom/pan any panel to inspect; the view mirrors
across all panels.
